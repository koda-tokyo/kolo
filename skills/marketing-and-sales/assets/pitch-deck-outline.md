# Pitch Deck Outline — KodaSōken / KODA KENKŌ

12-slide structure for investor presentations. Each slide has one job.

---

## Slide 1: Title
**Job:** Who you are, in 5 seconds.
- Company name + logo
- One-liner: "[What we do] for [who]"
- Presenter name, title, contact

---

## Slide 2: The Problem
**Job:** Make them feel the pain. Be specific.
- 3 bullet points MAX
- Quantify: "Japanese doctors spend X hours/day on documentation"
- Make it personal: "That's X hours not spent with patients, family, or sleep"
- Cite a source (MHLW survey, industry report)

---

## Slide 3: The Market
**Job:** Show this is big and structured.
- TAM: Total hospital beds / clinics in Japan
- SAM: Addressable segment (e.g., hospitals with EMR, 300+ beds)
- SOM: Realistic near-term target
- Key metric: beds × ¥/bed/year, not "aging population narrative"
- Show growth driver (2024 医師働き方改革 as catalyst)

---

## Slide 4: Our Solution
**Job:** Show what you built, simply.
- Product screenshot or diagram (not feature list)
- One sentence describing what it does
- Before/after workflow comparison (visual, not text-heavy)
- "It works like this:" — 3 steps max

---

## Slide 5: Why Now
**Job:** Timing isn't luck.
- Regulatory catalyst (働き方改革, 診療報酬改定)
- Technology catalyst (mature Japanese NLP, on-prem AI feasible)
- Market catalyst (hospital digitization post-COVID)
- "The window is open. It won't stay open forever."

---

## Slide 6: The Moat
**Job:** Why competitors can't easily copy this.
- Use the moat map: Japanese NLP → MHLW compliance → on-prem deployment → multi-product → relationships
- Not "we're better" — "this is structurally hard to replicate"
- Regulatory barrier is the strongest moat in healthcare

---

## Slide 7: Traction
**Job:** Prove it's real.
- Deployments: X hospitals, Y beds covered
- Metrics: documentation time reduced by Z%, user satisfaction
- Pipeline: X hospitals in evaluation
- If early stage: pilot results, LOIs, partner commitments

---

## Slide 8: Business Model
**Job:** Show how money works.
- Revenue model: per-bed/year, per-hospital, SaaS + implementation
- Unit economics: CAC, LTV, gross margin, payback period
- Pricing: ¥X/bed/year or ¥Y/hospital/year
- "At scale: X hospitals × ¥Y = ¥Z revenue"

---

## Slide 9: Go-to-Market
**Job:** How you reach customers.
- Channel: direct sales, SIer partners, medical device distributors
- Sales cycle: typical length, who signs, who influences
- "We acquire hospitals through [channel] at ¥X CAC"
- Partnership strategy if applicable

---

## Slide 10: Roadmap
**Job:** Where this goes.
- Next 12 months: product milestones, hospital targets, certification timeline
- Next 24 months: product expansion, geographic expansion
- Long-term vision: the full KODA KENKŌ product ecosystem
- PMDA/SaMD certification timeline if applicable

---

## Slide 11: Team
**Job:** Why this team wins.
- Founders/leads: name, role, relevant credential (1 line each)
- Healthcare domain expertise
- Technical expertise
- Advisory board / key relationships
- "This team has both the healthcare credibility and technical capability"

---

## Slide 12: The Ask
**Job:** Clear next step.
- "We're raising ¥X million / $X million"
- Use of funds: Y% product, Z% sales, W% regulatory
- Target close date
- Contact information
- "We'd love to schedule a deeper dive"

---

## Rules for the Deck

1. **One idea per slide.** If a slide has two ideas, split it.
2. **No slide should need more than 30 seconds to understand.**
3. **Numbers > adjectives.** "60% reduction" beats "dramatic improvement."
4. **Visual > text.** Diagrams, charts, screenshots. Not bullet walls.
5. **Japanese and English versions.** Japanese for domestic VCs, English for global.
6. **Appendix slides** for detailed financials, tech architecture, clinical studies — have them ready, don't put them in the main deck.
