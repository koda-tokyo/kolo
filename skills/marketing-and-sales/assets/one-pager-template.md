## One-Pager Rules
1. **One page.** If it doesn't fit, cut. Not smaller font — fewer words.
2. **One audience primary.** If you try to speak to everyone, you speak to no one. Pick the primary reader.
3. **Benefits, not features.** Every feature must answer "so what?" for the reader.
4. **Japanese and English versions.** Not translation — adaptation. Different proof points for different markets.
5. **White space is not empty.** It's the reader's breathing room. Don't fill it.
