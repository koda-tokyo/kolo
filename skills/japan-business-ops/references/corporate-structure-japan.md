## 1. KK (株式会社) Overview

The 株式会社 (Kabushiki Kaisha / KK) is the standard Japanese stock corporation under the Companies Act (会社法).

### Key Characteristics
- Separate legal person (法人) distinct from shareholders
- Limited liability for shareholders
- Minimum capital: ¥1 (effectively; practical minimum ¥1M+ for credibility)
- At least one director (取締役) required
- Can be private (非公開会社) or public (公開会社)
- Foreign nationals can serve as directors with no residency requirement

### KK vs Other Corporate Forms

| | KK (株式会社) | GK (合同会社) |
|---|---|---|
| Credibility | High — standard form | Lower — perceived as "simpler KK" |
| Setup Cost | ¥240,000+ (registration tax) | ¥60,000+ |
| Setup Time | 4-6 weeks | 3-4 weeks |
| Governance | Board + shareholders meeting required | Flexible; member-managed |
| Investors | Preferred by VCs and institutional | Rarely accepted |
| Foreign Business | Standard choice | OK for small subsidiaries |

**Recommendation:** Use KK for any business that will contract with Japanese enterprises or government. GK is perceived as less credible.

## 2. Board of Directors Requirements

### Director Structure Options

| Structure | Directors | Terms | Best For |
|-----------|-----------|-------|----------|
| No Board (取締役会非設置) | 1+ director | Up to 10 years | Small subsidiaries |
| With Board (取締役会設置) | 3+ directors | Up to 2 years | Standard KK |
| With Committees (委員会設置会社) | 3+ committees | Complex | Public companies |

### Director Duties (会社法 Art. 330, 355)
- Duty of care (善管注意義務)
- Duty of loyalty (忠実義務)
- Duty to avoid competitive business (競業避止義務) — board approval required
- Duty to avoid conflict-of-interest transactions (利益相反取引規制) — board approval required

### Board Meeting Requirements
- Convened by representative director (代表取締役)
- Notice: At least 1 week (can be shortened by unanimous consent)
- Quorum: Majority of directors (unless articles specify higher)
- Resolutions: Majority of attending directors
- Minutes required (議事録); stored for 10 years at head office
- Can be held virtually (online) if articles permit

### Representative Director (代表取締役)
- Has authority to bind the company externally
- Must be registered (登記)
- At least one required if board exists
- Can be multiple representative directors
- Foreign national: allowed; no residency requirement; practical banking challenges

## 3. Shareholder Meetings (株主総会)

### Annual Meeting (定時株主総会)
- Must be held within 3 months after fiscal year end
- Approve: financial statements, director elections, dividends
- Notice: 2 weeks before meeting (1 week for private companies)
- Convened by board (or director if no board)

### Extraordinary Meeting (臨時株主総会)
- Held as needed for significant matters
- Same notice requirements

### Resolutions
| Type | Vote Required | Typical Matters |
|------|---------------|-----------------|
| Ordinary | Majority of voting rights present (quorum: majority of total voting rights) | Director election, dividends |
| Special | 2/3+ of voting rights present (quorum: majority of total) | Articles amendment, merger, dissolution, capital reduction |

### Shareholder Rights (Key Articles)
- Right to inspect accounting books (Art. 433) — 3%+ shareholder
- Right to propose agenda items (Art. 303-305) — 1%+ or 300+ voting units
- Right to file derivative action (Art. 847) — 1%+ for 6 months
- Right to demand director dismissal (Art. 854) — 1/3+ or 3% for 6 months

### Practical Considerations for Foreign Owners
- Minutes in Japanese (English translation acceptable as supplementary)
- Shareholder meeting can be held overseas if articles permit
- Proxy voting allowed; written consent for private companies
- Dividend distribution to foreign shareholders: 20.42% withholding tax (treaty may reduce)

## 4. Corporate Seals (実印・銀行印)

### Seal Types

| Seal | Japanese | Registration | Use |
|------|----------|-------------|-----|
| Registered Seal | 実印 (Jitsuin) | Registered at Legal Affairs Bureau | Contracts, real estate, notarized documents |
| Bank Seal | 銀行印 (Ginkōin) | Registered at bank | Bank transactions, account opening |
| Company Seal | 角印 (Kakuin) | Not registered | Invoices, quotations, routine documents |

### Seal Management
- 実印 is the most legally significant seal
- Certificate of Seal Impression (印鑑証明書) validates 実印 — valid 3 months
- Seal management is a governance matter — who holds which seal
- Lost 実印 = must report to Legal Affairs Bureau and re-register
- Digital signatures are increasingly accepted but 実印 remains standard for high-value contracts
- Foreign companies with Japan branch: representative's personal 実印 often used

### Seal Certificate (印鑑証明書) Acquisition
1. Register 実印 at Legal Affairs Bureau (会社法人等番号 required)
2. Obtain 印鑑カード (seal card)
3. Print 印鑑証明書 at convenience store or obtain at Legal Affairs Bureau
4. Validity: 3 months from issuance

## 5. Statutory Filings Calendar

### Annual Filing Cycle (Typical March year-end)

| Month | Filing | Authority | Deadline |
|-------|--------|-----------|----------|
| Within 3 months of FY end | Annual Shareholders Meeting | Internal | — |
| Within 3 months of FY end | Financial Statement Filing (計算書類公告) | Legal Affairs Bureau | — |
| Within 2 months of FY end | Corporate Tax Return (法人税申告) | Tax Office | +1 month extension possible |
| Within 2 months of FY end | Consumption Tax Return (消費税申告) | Tax Office | — |
| Within 2 months of FY end | Local Corporate Tax Returns (地方税) | Prefectural/Municipal | — |
| Annually (formation date anniversary) | Corporate Registry Update (変更登記) | Legal Affairs Bureau | Within 2 weeks of any change |
| Annually (April-May) | Fixed Asset Tax Return (償却資産申告) | Municipal | January 31 |
| Annually (July) | Labor Insurance Annual Update (労働保険年度更新) | Labour Bureau | July 10 |
| Annually (June-July) | Social Insurance Base Amount Notification (算定基礎届) | Pension Office | July 10 |

### Trigger-Based Filings (期限付き)
| Event | Filing | Deadline |
|-------|--------|----------|
| Director change | 変更登記 | 2 weeks |
| Capital increase | 変更登記 | 2 weeks |
| Head office relocation | 変更登記 | 2 weeks |
| Articles amendment | 変更登記 | 2 weeks |
| Issuing new shares | 変更登記 | 2 weeks if beyond authorized |

### Late Filing Penalties
- Corporate registry: ¥1M max fine (過料) for director negligence
- Tax filings: penalty tax (加算税) + interest (延滞税)
- Persistent non-filing: risk of forced dissolution

## 6. Corporate Governance Code

### Applicability
- Tokyo Stock Exchange-listed companies: mandatory "comply or explain"
- Unlisted companies: not legally binding but increasingly referenced as best practice

### Key Principles (for unlisted companies adopting best practice)
1. **Secure shareholder rights** — Equal treatment, exercise environment
2. **Appropriate cooperation with stakeholders** — Employees, customers, creditors, community
3. **Appropriate information disclosure** — Transparency and accountability
4. **Board responsibilities** — Strategic direction, oversight, fiduciary duties
5. **Dialogue with shareholders** — Constructive engagement

### Practical Governance for Small/Medium KK
- Written board resolutions (取締役会議事録) maintain even if meetings are informal
- Conflict-of-interest transaction documentation
- Separation of management oversight and business execution (where possible)
- Related-party transaction disclosure (tax and governance implications)
- Document retention: 10 years for board/shareholder minutes at minimum

## 7. Auditor vs Committee Structures

### Three Governance Models

| Model | Japanese | Structure | Best For |
|-------|----------|-----------|----------|
| Statutory Auditor | 監査役設置会社 | 1+ 監査役 (statutory auditor) | Most unlisted KK; simpler |
| Audit & Supervisory Committee | 監査等委員会設置会社 | 3+ directors on audit committee | Listed companies seeking flexibility |
| Three Committees | 委員会設置会社 | Nominating, Audit, Compensation committees | Large listed companies |

### Statutory Auditor (監査役 — Kansayaku)
- Not a "committee" — individual(s) with statutory role
- Audits director performance of duties (業務監査)
- Attends board meetings; right to speak
- Can investigate company operations and finances
- Cannot be also a director (must be independent)
- Term: 4 years (vs 2 years for directors)
- At least 1 required for companies with board of directors
- Private company: can be 1 kansayaku; public: 3+ kansayaku

### Audit & Supervisory Committee Model
- Board committee replaces statutory auditor
- Committee members must be directors (unlike kansayaku)
- Majority of committee members must be outside directors
- Company can delegate significant authority to executive directors (flexibility)

### Choosing a Structure
- **Small subsidiary (<50 employees):** Single statutory auditor
- **Medium enterprise (50-300 employees):** One statutory auditor, or audit committee
- **Preparing for investment:** Statutory auditor acceptable; committee structures preferred by institutional investors
- **Planning IPO:** Audit committee or three-committee model

## 8. Branch Office vs Subsidiary

### Branch Office (支店 / 日本支店)
- Extension of foreign parent (not separate legal entity)
- Registration: register foreign company (外国会社登記)
- Representative in Japan required (residency: practical expectation though not legal requirement)
- Parent company directly liable for branch obligations
- Tax: only Japan-source income taxed
- Setup: 4-8 weeks; ¥90,000+ registration tax
- Simpler closure

### Subsidiary (子会社 — KK/GK)
- Separate Japanese legal entity
- Parent liability limited to investment
- Full Japanese corporate tax on worldwide (Japan-sourced) income
- More credible to Japanese customers/government
- Required for government contracting qualification (入札参加資格)
- Setup: 4-6 weeks (KK); ¥240,000+ registration tax

### Decision Matrix
| Factor | Branch Office | KK Subsidiary |
|--------|---------------|---------------|
| Government contracts | Difficult | Required |
| Enterprise credibility | Lower | Higher |
| Setup cost | Lower | Higher |
| Operational complexity | Lower | Higher |
| Parent company liability | Unlimited | Limited |
| Banking | More difficult | Standard |
| Closure | Simple | Complex |

**For any serious Japan market entry involving government or large enterprise customers:** KK subsidiary is the correct choice.
