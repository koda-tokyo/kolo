# Legal/Corporate Japanese — 法務日本語

## The Language of Law, Contracts, and Corporate Governance

Legal Japanese is the most formal and archaic register of the modern language. It retains grammatical patterns from classical Japanese (文語) that have disappeared from everyday speech. For KODA KENKŌ, this is the language of 会社法 (Companies Act), contracts, MHLW regulatory compliance, 内容証明 (certified mail for legal notices), and corporate governance documents.

---

## The Grammar of Obligation — Legal Modality

Legal Japanese has a precise hierarchy of obligation that is more nuanced than English:

| Expression | Force | English Equivalent |
|------------|-------|-------------------|
| 〜しなければならない | Absolute duty | MUST (no discretion) |
| 〜するものとする | Prescriptive rule | SHALL |
| 〜しなければならないものとする | Reinforced MUST | MUST — emphasized |
| 〜してはならない | Absolute prohibition | MUST NOT |
| 〜することができる | Permission/capability | MAY |
| 〜することができない | Incapacity/prohibition | CANNOT / MAY NOT |
| 〜することを要する | Requirement | It is required that |
| 〜する義務を負う | Bears a duty to | SHALL be obligated to |
| 〜に努めるものとする | Best efforts | SHALL endeavor to |
| 〜を妨げない | Does not preclude | Nothing shall prevent |
| 〜ものとする。ただし、〜 | Rule with exception | SHALL... provided, however, that |

## Contract Structure — 契約書の構成

### Standard Articles (条文)

```
第1条 (目的)
本契約は、甲が乙に対して提供する医療AIシステムの開発及び運用に関し、
両当事者の権利義務を定めることを目的とする。

Article 1 (Purpose)
This Agreement sets forth the rights and obligations of the parties
with respect to the development and operation of the medical AI system
to be provided by Party A to Party B.

第2条 (定義)
本契約において、以下の用語は次の意味を有するものとする。
(1) 「本システム」とは、別紙1に記載の仕様を有する医療AIプログラムをいう。
(2) 「知的財産権」とは、特許権、実用新案権、意匠権、商標権、著作権...

Article 2 (Definitions)
In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:
(1) "The System" means the medical AI program having the specifications
    described in Exhibit 1.
(2) "Intellectual Property Rights" means patent rights, utility model rights,
    design rights, trademark rights, copyright...
```

### Standard Contract Clauses (for KODA KENKŌ)

```
第X条 (秘密保持)
甲及び乙は、本契約に関連して知り得た相手方の技術上又は営業上の情報
（以下「秘密情報」という）を、相手方の事前の書面による承諾なくして
第三者に開示又は漏洩してはならない。

Article X (Confidentiality)
Neither Party A nor Party B shall disclose or leak to any third party,
without the prior written consent of the other party, any technical or
business information of the other party obtained in connection with this
Agreement (hereinafter referred to as "Confidential Information").

第Y条 (損害賠償)
甲又は乙は、本契約に違反し、相手方に損害を生じさせたときは、
当該損害を賠償する責任を負うものとする。ただし、甲の賠償責任は、
本契約の契約金額を上限とする。

Article Y (Damages)
If Party A or Party B breaches this Agreement and causes damage to the
other party, it shall be liable to compensate such damage; provided,
however, that Party A's liability shall be capped at the contract amount.

第Z条 (準拠法及び管轄)
本契約は日本法に準拠し、日本法に従って解釈されるものとする。
本契約に関する一切の紛争については、東京地方裁判所を第一審の
専属的合意管轄裁判所とする。

Article Z (Governing Law and Jurisdiction)
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with
the laws of Japan. Any and all disputes arising out of or in connection
with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction
of the Tokyo District Court as the court of first instance.
```

## 内容証明 — Certified Content Mail

The `内容証明郵便` (naishōmei yūbin) is Japan's formal legal notification system. The post office certifies: (1) who sent it, (2) to whom, (3) when, and (4) what the content was. It is the standard first step in Japanese legal disputes.

### Standard Opening
```
内容証明郵便

拝啓

前略、本書をもって、下記の通りご通知申し上げます。

（This is to notify you, by means of this document, of the following:）
```

### Standard Closing
```
つきましては、本書到達後14日以内に、下記連絡先まで
書面にてご回答くださいますようお願い申し上げます。
なお、同期間内にご回答いただけない場合には、
法的手続きを含む必要な措置を取らざるを得ないことを
申し添えます。

(Accordingly, please respond in writing to the address below
within 14 days of receipt of this notice. Should no response
be received within said period, we must add that we will be
compelled to take necessary measures, including legal proceedings.)

敬具
```

### The 14-Day Standard
Japanese legal practice conventionally uses 14 days as the notice period for demand letters. This is not statutory (statutory periods vary by claim type) but is so standard that departing from it signals either unusual urgency or bad faith.

## Reading Statutes — 法令読解

### The Structure of Japanese Laws
```
法律名: 医療分野における人工知能の利用促進に関する法律
Law name: Act on Promotion of AI Utilization in the Medical Field

（目的）
第一条 この法律は、医療分野における人工知能の適正かつ
効果的な利用を促進するため、基本的な事項を定めるものとする。

(Purpose)
Article 1. This Act shall establish fundamental matters for the purpose
of promoting appropriate and effective utilization of artificial intelligence
in the medical field.

（定義）
第二条 この法律において「医療AI」とは、医療分野において
使用される人工知能技術の総称をいう。

(Definitions)
Article 2. In this Act, "Medical AI" means the generic term for artificial
intelligence technology used in the medical field.
```

### Key Reading Conventions
```
1. 条 (jō) = Article, 項 (kō) = Paragraph, 号 (gō) = Item
   第10条第3項第2号 = Article 10, Paragraph 3, Item 2

2. Parenthetical definitions:
   医療AI（以下「対象技術」という）
   Medical AI (hereinafter referred to as "the Subject Technology")

3. "この法律において" = scope limiter: "For the purposes of this Act..."
   A definition may differ from the ordinary meaning of the word.

4. "主務大臣" = the competent minister (whoever is in charge of this domain)
   This is a Japanese legal term meaning "whichever minister(s) have jurisdiction"
   — typically MHLW for medical matters, METI for technology, etc.
```

## The Language of 印鑑 (Seals)

Japan's seal system is legally significant, not ceremonial:

| Term | Significance |
|------|-------------|
| 実印 (jitsuin) | Registered seal — filed at city hall, used for major contracts |
| 印鑑証明書 (inkan shōmeisho) | Seal registration certificate — proves the 実印 is genuine |
| 銀行印 (ginkōin) | Bank seal — registered with financial institution |
| 認印 (mitomein) | Unregistered seal — for daily use, less legal weight |
| 記名押印 (kimei ōin) | Name + seal (seal alone is not enough; must have name) |
| 署名捺印 (shomei natsuin) | Signature + seal (strongest — signature is primary, seal is additional) |

Corporate documents typically require: 記名押印 (company name written + representative director's seal). Some critical documents require 署名捺印 (personal signature + seal — harder to forge).
