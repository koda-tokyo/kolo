# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone who takes part in this project (issues, discussions, pull requests, documentation, chat) is expected to:

- be respectful and considerate in language and action,
- assume good faith and ask before concluding,
- accept constructive review and give it in the same spirit,
- keep disagreements about the work, not the person,
- respect the privacy of others; never publish someone's private information.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, sexualised language or imagery, threats, sustained disruption, or publishing private data.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to **conduct@kodasoken.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that violate this standard, and may temporarily or permanently exclude participants for repeated or serious violations.
